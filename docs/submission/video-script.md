# 30-second video script (Field Shift, team Project: Hail Mary)

Patterns borrowed from the Meta hackathon winners (see `winners-video-teardown.md`):
- A plain first-person hook.
- One problem, one turn, one answer.
- Real screens, not stock footage.
- Burned-in captions.
- Light music, which drops under the key line.

Cairo rules:
- The video is 30 s or less and public.
- No faces or voices of anyone under 18.
- English subtitles.

**About 72 words of narration** at about 2.5 words a second. Time the read on the day, and cut words rather than speed it up.

| Time | Picture | Narration (captions identical) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | Satellite view of the Delta, slowly zooming into a patchwork of fields | "Every farmer in the Delta hears it: plant clover first, and your rice grows better." |
| 0:04–0:09 | The crop map flips season by season, 2016 → 2025 (wheat, clover, rice colours) | "We tracked 100,000 fields across ten years with NASA satellites." |
| 0:09–0:13 | Bar chart: rice after clover is taller | "At first, it looked true." |
| 0:13–0:21 | Field calendar strip: the May–June band lights red ("clover still green"), then a split screen of the same field over two years | "But farmers give clover their best land, and clover stays green into June. The satellite was fooled twice." |
| 0:21–0:26 | The bars become level. Caption: "measured on 17,000 fields" | "Remove both illusions, and the boost disappears." **(music drops)** |
| 0:26–0:30 | App: tap a field → "10 fields like yours" → a one-line answer. End card: *Precrop Payoff Atlas · NASA HLS · ECOSTRESS* | "Our tool shows farmers which advice really holds up on land like theirs." |

## Production checklist
- **Screens:**
  - Record the app with a cursor-zoom screen recorder (Screen Studio on Mac; Recordly, Cap or Focusee on Windows).
  - Script each take, as ORCA did, so retakes match.
- **Voice:**
  - Your own voice works best: five of the six winners used a narrator, most in the first person ("I", "we"). Formwright used music and on-screen text only.
  - The fallback is a TTS voice (ElevenLabs or similar), which must be disclosed under the rules' AI-use note.
- **Captions:**
  - Auto-generate them (CapCut, Descript or DaVinci Resolve), then correct the words by hand.
  - Burn them in, because judges watch muted.
- **Edit:**
  - Use CapCut or DaVinci Resolve (both free): a 1920×1080 timeline, export at 1080p.
- **Music:** royalty-free (YouTube Audio Library), low under the voice, and none under 0:21–0:26.
- **Before upload:** check the video is ≤ 30.0 s, public or unlisted-public, and opens logged-out.
